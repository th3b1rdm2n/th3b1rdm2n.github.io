This space marks the beginning of something I’ve been quietly crafting — deliberately, patiently, and with a deepening commitment to mastery. If you’re drawn to technology, purposeful living, and skillful growth — you’re in the right place. 

This space is grounded in **integrity, simplicity, and depth**, it's for those who believe that personal growth, intentional living, and technology are intertwined, and that each should be **mastered, maintained, and monitored**.

---

## 🧠 Who I Am

I’m a **security researcher** with a background in **infrastructure engineering** — designing resilient systems, deploying applications at scale, and building observability into everything.

Over time, my focus shifted beyond system performance and availability to exploring **where systems fail**, **what makes them break**, and **how they can be abused**.

> That transition wasn't just technical; it was philosophical.
> To master anything — a system, a skill, or the self — you have to understand how it **works**, how it **breaks**, and how it **endures**.

I approach my work and life with the same principles: depth over noise, resilience over flash, and clarity over chaos. I live by stoic ideals — purpose, discipline, and self-mastery — and bring those into every domain I engage with, whether it's building secure systems or developing inner strength.

What I bring to the table is technical precision, philosophical grounding, and a relentless drive to build capacity — in systems, in people, and in myself.

My name isn’t one you’ve seen on conference stages — yet.
But behind the screen, I’ve been quietly sharpening my edge — guided by the same relentless curiosity that fuels minds like [James Kettle](https://jameskettle.com/), [Orange Tsai](https://blog.orange.tw/), [Kinugawa Mataso](https://masatokinugawa.l0.cm/), and [Huli](https://blog.huli.tw/).
