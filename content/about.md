Hi, 👋🏽 I'm a **security researcher** with a background in **infrastructure engineering** — designing reliable systems, deploying robust applications, and ensuring the platforms they run on are observable and resilient.

For years, I worked at the intersection of development and operations — making systems run reliably, from bare metal to container clusters — wielding automation like a sculptor shapes stone.

Then I turned my focus to the **security side of software engineering** — driven by a need to **see beneath the surface**: to understand how systems break, why they fail, and how adversaries think.

---

## 🔍 What I Do

My work focuses on:

- **Vulnerability research & disclosure**
- **System behavior analysis under stress**
- **Security tooling and automation**
- **Teaching and explaining technical concepts simply**
- **Designing defenses that scale**

I have a passion for understanding how systems break — and helping others understand how to protect them. My approach is analytical but deeply human. I believe in teaching—not just showing, but explaining.

---

## 🧠 How I Think

I believe in clarity over complexity. I approach security research the same way I teach:

> Break down big ideas.  
> Explain them clearly.  
> Show others how to build safer systems.

Whether I'm walking a team through a complex vulnerability or breaking down a concept for readers, I always aim to make the complex feel simple.
